Social Work

Shri Rani Sati Dadiji Temple : Social Work

This temple, a symbol of dedication, devotion, and service, has for years conveyed a message of spiritual energy and cultural unity to devotees.

Service and Social Work

1. Educational Support: Financial assistance is provided for the education of economically weaker children.

2. Medical Assistance: Medicines are provided to needy patients.

3. Safety अभियान: Free helmets were distributed.

4. Tree Plantation: Plantation programs are organized from time to time under the tree plantation initiative.

5. Women Support: In the presence of Honorable Home Minister Shri Harsh Bhai Sanghvi, free ration kits were distributed to 1100 widow sisters and are distributed from time to time.

6. Educational Infrastructure: Four classrooms were built in the school located at Pani Village, Palsana (Surat).

7. Accessibility Facility: A lift facility has been provided for visiting the temple.

8. Food Service: Food is served to the poor on every Amavasya.

9. Health Service: A homeopathic clinic has been running continuously for the last 23 years. Now it is open both morning and evening, benefiting 700–800 people per month.

10. Saturday Service: Every Saturday, food is served to the poor at Shani Dev Temple, Udhna–Magdalla Road.

11. Drinking Water Facility: Drinking water has been arranged for passersby on Golden Park Building Road.

Social Work

The construction journey of Shri Rani Sati Dadiji Temple, Surat, is an inspiring story. It began from a simple place where a few devotees gathered to perform kirtan and worship. In the early days, the temple did not have any permanent structure.
