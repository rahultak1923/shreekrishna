Shri Rani Sati Dadiji Temple : A Saga of History and Glory

This temple, a symbol of dedication, devotion, and service, has for years conveyed a message of spiritual energy and cultural unity to devotees.

Surat Rani Sati Temple (Shaktidham)

Shri Rani Sati Dadiji Temple : A Saga of History and Glory
  • 1. Historical Origin: The foundation of the temple is based on the historic sacrifice of Mata Narayani Devi (Rani Sati) in Samvat 1352.
  • 2. Tale of Bravery: Mata Narayani and her husband Tandhan Das Ji bravely fought against injustice.
  • 3. Divine Instruction: As per Mata’s guidance, where her horse “Shyam” stopped, the main Shakti Peeth was established in Jhunjhunu.
  • 4. Glory of 13 Satis: This temple is dedicated to the sacred memory of 13 Satis of the clan including Narayani Devi.
  • 5.Arrival in Surat: The Marwari community of Surat formed Shri Rani Sati Dadiji Temple Seva Samiti to promote the teachings of Dadiji.
  • 6.First Jagran (1991): The grand Jagran organized in 1991 inspired the construction of the temple in Surat.
  • 7.Foundation (1993): With the support and dedication of devotees, the foundation stone of the temple was laid in 1993.
  • 8.Construction (1994): With public support and offerings of “Tan-Man-Dhan,” the grand temple was completed in 1994.
  • 9.Collective Effort: This temple is a living symbol of the unwavering faith and unity of the devotees of Surat.
  • 10.Service is Religion: The main objective of the temple is public welfare and charity along with spiritual power.
  • 11.Health Service: A free homeopathic clinic has been continuously running here for the last 23 years.
  • 12.Medical Benefit: Every month, 700 to 800 needy people benefit from this clinic.
  • 13.Educational Support: The committee continuously provides financial assistance for the education of economically weaker children.
  • 14.Infrastructure: The committee has built four classrooms in a school in Palsana (Surat).
  • 15.Food Donation: Food is served to the poor on every Amavasya and service is provided at Shani Temple on Saturdays.
  • 16.Women Empowerment: Free ration kits are distributed to around 1100 widowed mothers from time to time.
  • 17.Environment & Safety: Free helmet distribution and large-scale tree plantation drives are conducted in public interest.
  • 18.Prime Minister’s Appreciation: In 2002, former Prime Minister Atal Bihari Vajpayee praised the temple’s service activities.
  • 19.Spiritual Message: “Keep your feelings pure, keep your mind clean” – this teaching of Dadiji is the core mantra of the temple.
  • 20.Unshakable Faith: Today, Shaktidham Surat is not just a temple, but a major center for service to humanity.

Journey of Construction

The construction journey of Shri Rani Sati Dadiji Temple, Surat, is an inspiring story. It began from a simple place where a few devotees gathered to perform kirtan and worship. In the early days, the temple did not have any permanent structure.
